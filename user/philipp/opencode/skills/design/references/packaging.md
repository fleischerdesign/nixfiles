# Packaging

## When to load & job

Lead with Packaging for printed artwork on a carton, label, sleeve or pouch. Design for choosing the right pack in a row and then turning it to find the details. Deliver a panel study if the structure is unresolved, or editable artwork and the converter's requested files when the dieline and print conditions are supplied. Brand defines the identity system; Print supports file exchange but does not decide how information is distributed around the object. Construction and material approval are outside this job.

## Inputs & concepts

Get shelf or listing context, near-neighbor variants, the SKU and market matrix, source assets and copy approved by the product and regulatory owners. Ask who supplies the barcode artwork and which scanning environment applies. Get the converter's issued dieline and print specification, including revision, viewing side, bleed, exclusion zones, materials and ink or finish instructions. Without a dieline, label the face geometry as assumed and leave production artwork open.

The **dieline** describes where the structure cuts, folds, joins or seals; the **principal display face** here means the face presented to shoppers, not a claim about the legal definition of a panel. A **usable face** excludes areas lost to overlaps or supplier allowances, even if those areas are visible in the artwork file. On a folded object, adjacent panels can have different reading tops. **Separations** express what prints or receives a finish; a drawing of a crease is structural information, not itself an ink plate. A barcode's **quiet zones** are reserved parts of the symbol footprint, not spare graphic space.

## Decisions & procedure

- **Family first or variant first.** Let a recognizable product image lead if the shopper is choosing a kind of product. For close flavors or strengths, reserve a fixed place and prominence for the variant name; that leaves less room for the image. Compare nearest neighbors at shelf scale.
- **One face or a wrap.** Let a background cross an edge if continuity helps the pack read as one object. Keep copy that must read in a glance on one face, even if it takes space from the illustration.
- **Shared or market-specific artwork.** Reserve space using the supplied copy before sizing images. A shared pack reduces versions, but separate artwork can give long language blocks more room. Put the heaviest copy variant beside the shortest one.

1. Translate the issued geometry into a **numbered panel map and copy budget**. Use [Packaging artwork](knowledge/packaging-artwork.md) (`references/knowledge/packaging-artwork.md`) to establish reading tops, visible faces, copy allocation and the separation schedule. Use the map to decide which faces should answer choice, identification and detail questions.
2. Compose the closest variants first and put them in a shelf lineup. Mark what changes between SKUs and what stays fixed. Carry the busiest version around the folded study; move or reduce art before asking the copy owner about changes to required content.
3. Produce the flat and folded view from the same artwork. Use [Color management](knowledge/color-management.md) (`references/knowledge/color-management.md`) to specify substrate-dependent ink, finish and proof decisions. If PDF is the exchange format, use [Print PDF](knowledge/print-pdf.md) (`references/knowledge/print-pdf.md`) for the converter's export requirements; page boxes do not replace the agreed structural layer.

## Worked example & typical mistakes

Two similar tea cartons are displayed with a narrow side facing out. On the panel map, that side becomes the choice face: repeat the full variant name there in the same position on both packs, although the illustrated wide face stays the hero in the online listing. Put brewing directions on a face reached by one turn, then use the remaining uninterrupted face for the supplied copy and barcode footprint. The lineup now shows the distinction shoppers actually see, and the folded study explains where they find the details.

Centering an ingredients statement across two panels can balance the flat but hide part of the sentence around the corner. Keep the statement on one usable face and carry only the background across the crease.

## Standards, limits & handover

[GS1 General Specifications, release 26.0](https://ref.gs1.org/standards/genspecs/26.0.0) covers barcode applications (§2), symbol specifications (§5) and placement (§6); `verify` the applicable release and provisions. It does not decide general labeling obligations. Adobe Illustrator Help's [“About color”](https://helpx.adobe.com/nz/illustrator/using/color.html), “Spot and process colors,” describes tool behavior; `verify` the installed version. The converter supplies physical allowances and separation names; fixed variant-name placement is a system convention.

Show the shelf lineup, annotated flat and folded preview. Ask which variants are most easily confused and which face the shelf exposes. Request a physical sample from the converter; a mockup cannot establish fold behavior, registration or scanner performance. Send market copy questions to the regulatory owner rather than inferring requirements from another pack.
