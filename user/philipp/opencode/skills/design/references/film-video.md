# Film & Video

## When to load & job

Use this for a storyboard, animatic, or edit direction. Your material is the sequence: what viewers learn from one shot, what they infer from the next, and how long those ideas have to land. This is previz, not a shoot or finished film.

## Inputs

Bring the script or interview selects, available footage, intended audience, and delivery brief. Take runtime, aspect ratios, and version requirements from the producer or distributor. Without footage, use labeled boards or placeholders; without delivery specs, state the working runtime and frame shape. Leave final shot selection and versioning until those inputs arrive.

## Decisions & process

- **Orient or withhold.** Open wide when geography matters to the action. Enter on a detail when curiosity should lead, but give the audience a later shot that locates it. Board both openings through the same reveal; the first shot alone won’t show what each version gives away.
- **Stay with the action or cut to its consequence.** Hold through a gesture if the way it happens tells the story. Cut to the person receiving it when their reaction changes its meaning; you give up the end of the gesture to see the consequence sooner.
- **Pace by thought or by impact.** Let an explanation finish over the next image when the audience needs time to connect the two. Use harder picture and sound cuts together for a deliberate rupture; repeated too often, they give every beat the same emphasis.

## Typical mistakes & examples

An interview cut lands on every pause, filling each gap with a new angle. Half-formed thoughts start to sound like finished claims. Hold on the speaker through the turn in their thought, then use a cutaway where the subject actually changes.

## Deliverables & questions for the human

Show a timed animatic or paper edit with shot IDs, dialogue, and temp sound marked. Ask what the viewer should know before the reveal and which moment deserves the longest hold. The editor’s next proof is a rough cut from actual footage; a producer resolves missing coverage.
