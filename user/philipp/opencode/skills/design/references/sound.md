# Sound

## When to load & job

Use this for a sound direction brief or a review of cues against picture or interaction. Describe what the listener should notice, anticipate, or feel. Leave recording, synthesis, editing, and the final mix to the audio team.

## Inputs

Bring a cut or interaction recording, dialogue and music references, and the listening context: headphones, phone speaker, quiet room, or busy public space. Take loudness and peak limits from the destination’s delivery spec. Without it, describe what should sit in front or behind by ear and mark delivery levels as unset; don’t invent a target.

## Decisions & process

- **Sync to contact or lead into it.** Anchor a confirmation cue to the completed action when timing communicates cause. Let an anticipatory sound start before a scene reveal when suspense matters, but avoid that lead for uncertain outcomes: a success cue heard before completion says the task is already done.
- **Use contrast or sustain a bed.** Pull music back around a line if the voice should feel exposed. Keep a quiet layer of room tone if a complete drop would sound like the audio cut out. Mark which sounds remain, not just where to “go silent.”
- **Carry detail or carry through.** A soft textural cue can suit close headphone listening; on small speakers in noise it may disappear. Ask the audio team to explore a more distinct attack or register before making it louder than the surrounding mix. A cue that travels well may feel more insistent in a quiet room.

## Typical mistakes & examples

Putting a sharp chime on every step of a form makes routine progress sound like a string of arrivals. The final completion cue has nothing left to distinguish it. Keep intermediate steps visually legible and reserve the chime for the outcome that matters.

## Deliverables & questions for the human

Show a cue sheet against timecode or named events: intent, sync point, relative prominence, and intentional gaps. Ask which cues must carry information and which should disappear on repeat use. The sound designer’s next proof is a temp track against picture or interaction; the mixer needs the delivery spec.
